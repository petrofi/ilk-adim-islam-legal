Local story and saves

The fictional phone, messages, images and recordings are bundled assets. Progress, clues, choices, notes and language/audio settings remain in app-private storage. The app does not read real contacts, SMS, calls, photos, microphone, camera or GPS. Cloud and device-transfer backup are disabled. Gameplay is available at every real-world time, including offline. No trusted-time server or reviewer login is required.

Google advertising and data

This version integrates Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) 25.5.0 and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) 4.0.0. When permitted, Google may receive IP address and network/device information, advertising ID and app-set or other applicable device/account identifiers, app/ad interactions, and diagnostics such as crashes, performance and launch information. IP addresses may be used to estimate approximate location. Google processes these data for advertising, measurement/analytics and fraud prevention. The game has no separate analytics or crash-upload service and no mediation adapters.

Consent and personalization

UMP checks privacy requirements at launch and presents applicable consent messages. Advertising requests are made only when UMP permits them. Depending on your choices and applicable rules, ads may be personalized, non-personalized or limited. Non-personalized ads can still process data for measurement and security. Where Google requires a privacy entry point, Settings contains Privacy Options to review choices. Android settings also let you reset/delete the advertising identifier. Declining or failing consent does not remove gameplay access.

Ad placement and network

No App Open ad is shown on first use. Subsequent eligible openings may show one ready App Open ad over the loading screen, with a 30-minute cap. No delayed ad appears after reaching the menu, and no automatic interstitial appears during chapters, puzzles or recordings. Ad failures do not block the game. Google/UMP use encrypted network connections. This app requests INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, VIBRATE and advertising permissions contributed by the Google SDK; it does not request location permission.

Third parties, retention and deletion

Google is a third-party advertising/privacy service provider and its privacy policies and retention practices apply, including international processing where applicable. We do not promise that Google keeps information only briefly or that clearing game data deletes Google-held data. Uninstalling or clearing Android app storage deletes local saves/preferences; New Game resets story progress while keeping settings. Contact darkloveai@gmail.com for publisher-held support correspondence or privacy questions. Google policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy ; advertising information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads .

Older versions and this website

Earlier RC5 builds have no AdMob SDK and used a now-removed time admission system. This policy describes the updated build. The policy website contains no ads or tracking scripts; GitHub Pages may process visitor IP/request data under its own policies: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement . Future processing changes will be reflected here and in the Play disclosures.
