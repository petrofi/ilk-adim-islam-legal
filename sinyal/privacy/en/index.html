App and scope

This policy describes the Sinyal Android app (com.sinyal.mobile), published by darklove. Sinyal is a daily adventure app for a general audience. No account is required; Sinyal has no user account system or backend server of its own. The core experience runs on your device.

Data stored on your device

Sinyal uses Room and DataStore within Android app storage to provide its features and preserve your progress. Local data may include:

  • Profile information, Mino's name and customisation;
  • Mission progress, completed missions, XP, level and achievements;
  • Story progress, capsules, mood and personal notes;
  • An optional photo reference (URI), app settings and notification preferences.

This data is not sent to a Sinyal server. Local records may remain until you delete them or Android clears the app's data.

Capsules, notes and photos

Your capsule's mood, note and selected photo URI reference stay on your device. Photos are selected through Android's photo picker; the original file may remain with the selected media provider. Sinyal does not upload capsule text, personal notes, photos, photo URIs, Mino's name or mission history to its own server.

Sinyal does not send this content, XP or level to Google Ads as custom advertising targeting fields. Your chosen photo or storage provider also applies its own privacy practices.

Optional support advertising

Sinyal uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). An ad is prepared only after you choose Settings → Support Sinyal → Support by watching an ad and confirm; it is shown when the required conditions are met. This is a standard full-screen interstitial, without XP, rewards or feature unlocks.

There are no automatic ads at launch, mission start or completion, during navigation, when saving a capsule, or at random. Skipping an ad or an ad loading failure does not block the core experience.

Google's advertising services may process IP addresses, interactions, diagnostics and device/ad identifiers for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention under Google's own policies.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Mobile Ads data disclosure

Sinyal has no separately added user analytics or crash tracking service. The advertising SDK's own data processing is separate from this.

UMP and ad privacy choices

Sinyal uses Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). Where required, consent and advertising privacy choices use Google's interface. When supported, Settings → Ad privacy choices opens Google's privacy-options interface.

  • Google UMP information

Internet and external links

Missions, story, capsules, achievements and progress continue to work offline. Internet may be used to load optional ads, for Google Mobile Ads and UMP consent/privacy operations, and to open this policy in a browser. Your browser and the site you visit apply their own privacy practices when you open an external link.

Local notifications

Sinyal may use local reminders. You control notification permission; preferences stay on your device. Denying permission does not block core features. You can change your choice in the app or Android notification settings.

Backup and device transfer

Manual backup/restore in Settings includes only your profile and app settings. Mission records, capsules, notes, photos and story progress are not included in that file. The file is written to the location you choose through Android's picker; you control that location and any cloud storage provider you select.

The current Android cloud-backup rules exclude the database, DataStore directory and shared preferences; device-transfer rules permit those areas. Android or device-manufacturer backup and transfer behaviour may depend on the operating system and your device settings. These processes do not represent a Sinyal backend.

Deleting your data

Confirm Settings → Delete all data to erase Sinyal's app data on your device. Uninstalling also follows Android's normal app-data cleanup. Separately saved backup files, original photos and operating-system backups may need to be deleted separately.

In-app deletion does not erase data Google processes for advertising/consent; Google's privacy controls and request channels apply to that data. The publisher cannot remotely access or erase local data that stays on your device.

General audience

Sinyal offers a daily adventure experience for a general audience. This policy does not claim the app is designed specifically for a particular child age group or that minors' data is never processed under any circumstances. Young users should seek a parent or guardian's help with photos, external links and advertising choices when needed.

Security

Local records use Android's app sandbox and the app's existing storage mechanisms. Protecting your device and exported files matters. No storage or transmission method provides an absolute security guarantee.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this policy when app behaviour or services change. The current text is published here and the Last updated date above is revised.
